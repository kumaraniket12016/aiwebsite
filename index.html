GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

How often should I oil my hair? +

Once or twice a week is a lovely place to begin. Massage a small amount into your scalp and leave for at least 30 minutes before washing.

Are the oils suitable for all hair types? +

Our blends are designed for a wide range of hair types. Start with a small amount and adjust to what your hair enjoys.

How should I store my oil? +

Keep the bottle tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

How long does delivery take? +

Orders usually arrive within 3–6 business days across India.
